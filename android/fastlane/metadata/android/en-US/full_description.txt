<p>Gem Wallet is an open-source, non-custodial crypto wallet for 100+ blockchains. No registration or personal data is required: back up your recovery phrase and stay in control of your funds.</p>

<strong>Wallet</strong>
<ul>
    <li>Send and receive Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, USDT, USDC, BNB, XRP, TRON, TON, and many other assets.</li>
    <li>Manage USDT on TRON (TRC20), Ethereum (ERC20), Solana, TON, and more.</li>
    <li>Manage multiple wallets and accounts in one app.</li>
    <li>Add custom tokens and custom RPC nodes.</li>
    <li>View and send NFTs on popular blockchains.</li>
    <li>Find any asset quickly with search, and follow trending tokens and market data.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Swap, Earn, and Trade</strong>
<ul>
    <li>Swap tokens on the same chain or across chains through integrated providers such as Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Jupiter, Orca, THORChain, Chainflip, Mayan, Relay, and STON.fi.</li>
    <li>Stake supported assets and track your rewards.</li>
    <li>Earn with yield-bearing stablecoins.</li>
    <li>Trade Hyperliquid perpetuals.</li>
    <li>Buy and sell crypto through third-party providers.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Payments</strong>
<ul>
    <li>Pay with QR codes or from saved contacts.</li>
    <li>Only the blockchain network fee is charged on transfers.</li>
    <li>Review transaction details before signing.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Security and Privacy</strong>
<ul>
    <li>Private keys are encrypted and never leave your device.</li>
    <li>Protect access with a PIN and biometric authentication.</li>
    <li>Warnings for suspicious tokens and transactions.</li>
    <li>No registration or personal account is required.</li>
    <li>Open-source code is available for review.</li>
</ul>

<strong>F-Droid Build</strong>
<p>This build has no Google Play Services, push notifications, or WalletConnect.</p>
